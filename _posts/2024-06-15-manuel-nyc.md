![Salon in a mansion overlooking the Brooklyn Bridge]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2024-06-15-manuel-nyc.jpg)

This was one of the craziest weekends of my life. New York kept escalating, and I just kept saying yes.

I was lucky to be visiting my friends Julia and Lexi. Their dad Steve invited me to run a 10k with them, so my weekend started at full speed through Central Park.

![Running a 10k in Central Park]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2024-06-15-manuel-nyc-10k.jpg)

The city was in a strange mood that weekend. At one point I walked past an old man sunbathing on a park bench with mirrors, completely unbothered, tanning like it was his job. I should have taken it as a sign of what was coming.

![Old man sunbathing with mirrors on a park bench]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2024-06-15-manuel-nyc-sunbather.jpg)

Somewhere along the way I stumbled into a chess tournament in a pool hall. I sat down, played, and somehow came out second. That's where the weekend took its turn: through the tournament I got invited to a black-tie party.

![Chess tournament in a pool hall]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2024-06-15-manuel-nyc-chess.jpg)

I didn't own the clothes, so I ran to Zara for a suit and somehow got talked into an absurdly expensive belt at SuitSupply. Minutes before the event, my acquaintance texted me to say he wasn't coming.

![Black tie, minutes before walking in alone]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2024-06-15-manuel-nyc-blacktie.jpg)

I went anyway. The party was hosted by what I can only describe as a cult. I was the only non-Cantonese person in one of the most luxurious houses I've ever seen: grand piano, velvet sofas, crystal everywhere, a view of the Brooklyn Bridge. It was extremely fancy, with butlers moving around the rooms. I didn't dare eat or drink anything until I saw someone else do it first.

They pointed at the paintings and said they had just bought them from the Louvre, including a Monet. They explained that their organization, devoted to metaphysics and philosophy, would improve humanity. I smiled, nodded, and kept my glass untouched a little longer. I believe they launder money.

When I left, they offered me a driver to take me home. I was staying in a hostel with bunk beds. It was beyond real.

I didn't dare to talk to them again, but they kept writing to me.

Life is full of randomness.
