In 2015 I was volunteering in the mountains of Peru with AIESEC, traveling with my Dutch friend Loes. One day we lost our way. Night fell before we found a village, so we improvised a camp under a mosquito net, rocks for anchors, sleeping bags on the dirt.

![Emergency camp in the mountains]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2015-07-15-manuel-peru-camping.jpg)

We woke to farm workers with machetes. They were not happy to find strangers on their land. We bribed our way out, found the trail again, and still had nowhere to sleep, so we spent the next night in a police station.

![Morning in the Peruvian mountains]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2015-07-15-manuel-peru-valley.jpg)

The same trip had a softer chapter. While volunteering, I ended up in a full Mickey Mouse costume at a birthday party for a kid with a disability, kneeling in a room full of balloons and trying to make his afternoon special.

![Mickey Mouse at a birthday party]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2015-07-15-manuel-peru-mickey.jpg)
