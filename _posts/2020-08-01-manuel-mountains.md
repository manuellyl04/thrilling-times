This is the mountains chapter: two climbs, years apart, same hunger for altitude.

In **2017** I climbed **Ojos del Salado**—among the highest volcanoes on Earth—and stood on Andean rock with friends and the Uruguayan flag stretched between us, snow and cloud for a backdrop.

In **2020** I climbed **Kilimanjaro**. The trail wrote itself in camp signs and thin air: Karanga at 3,995 m, alpine desert underfoot, then the push toward Stella Point and Uhuru Peak.

![Karanga Camp, Kilimanjaro National Park]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2020-08-01-manuel-mountains-karanga.jpg)

Higher up, the mountain stopped looking like a postcard. Frost coated our jackets. The world went white. We still made it.
