Two climbs, years apart.

In **2017** I climbed **Ojos del Salado**, one of the highest volcanoes on Earth, and stood on Andean rock with friends, the Uruguayan flag stretched between us, snow and clouds behind.

In **2020** I climbed **Kilimanjaro**. Karanga Camp at 3,995 m, alpine desert underfoot, then the push toward Stella Point and Uhuru Peak.

![Karanga Camp, Kilimanjaro National Park]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2020-08-01-manuel-mountains-karanga.jpg)

Higher up it stopped looking like a postcard. Frost covered our jackets and everything went white. We still made it.
