In 2018, on a road near Florida, Uruguay, the van I was driving rolled. I was carrying a load of fire extinguishers for work (*ingeniería contra incendios*), not the kind of cargo you want flying around a cabin.

![The van on its roof]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/2018-06-15-manuel-florida-van.jpg)

The extinguishers broke the windows. None of them hit my head. That's the only reason I'm here to tell this. I almost didn't make it.
